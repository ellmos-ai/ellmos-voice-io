1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ellmos-voice-io provides local-first speech input (STT), speech output (TTS),
and wake-word helpers for LLM systems, agent runtimes, and desktop applications.
It is lightweight, LLM-neutral, and strictly zero-egress: it does not run a server,
store recordings, ship voice models, or require cloud providers.
